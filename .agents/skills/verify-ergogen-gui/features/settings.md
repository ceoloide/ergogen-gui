# Settings and feature flags

The settings change how the app generates and previews. The app stores each
switch when the user flips it, and keeps it after a reload. Two feature flags turn
custom outlines and custom templates on or off. By default they follow the
engine of the build, and the address can force them with `?ff_outlines=` and
`?ff_templates=`.

## Sub-features

- `settings-autogen` generates after each edit while `Auto-generate` is on,
  which is the default. While it is off, only `Generate` and the shortcut
  generate.
- `settings-autogen-3d` makes PCBs and cases in the automatic generation while
  `Auto-generate PCB & 3D` is on, which is the default. While it is off, the
  automatic generation leaves them out, and `Generate` still makes them.
- `settings-debug` adds debug files, such as `points.yaml`, to Outputs while
  `Debug` is on. See [Preview](./preview.md).
- `settings-kicad-preview` and `settings-stl-preview` show PCBs and cases in
  the preview while `KiCad Preview` and `STL Preview` are on.
- `settings-persist` keeps each switch after a reload.
- `flag-outlines` and `flag-templates` show the tabs `Outlines` and
  `Templates` in Custom Libraries and pass those injections to Ergogen when
  the engine is 4.3.0 or later. A build variable, or `?ff_outlines=true|false`
  and `?ff_templates=true|false` in the address, forces them. While a flag is
  off, the app hides the tab, skips those injections, and shows the warning
  `Custom outlines were skipped because the respective feature flags are disabled in settings.`,
  or the same warning for templates.
- `settings-metrics` and `settings-offline` are the features of analytics
  consent and of offline use.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `Show settings panel` in the header of the editor page. The switches
  are under `General` and `Previews (Experimental)`. Choose the title of a
  switch, such as `Debug`, to flip it.
- Add `?ff_outlines=false` or `?ff_templates=false` to the address of the app.
- A self-hoster sets `VITE_FEATURE_OUTLINES` or `VITE_FEATURE_TEMPLATES` for
  the build, as the [README](../../../../README.md) describes.

## Driving it with pnpm verify

Journey: `pnpm verify settings` runs `e2e/journeys/settings.journey.ts`.

Preconditions:

- A new browser context with empty storage at `/`, so each switch has its
  default and Custom Libraries is empty.
- Clipboard permissions, which the journey grants itself, for the paste.
- A build whose engine is 4.3.0 or later and with no `VITE_FEATURE_*`
  variable, which `pnpm verify` builds, so the flags are on by default.

- **Open the app.** Go to `/`. The heading `Ergogen Web UI` is visible.
- **Choose Empty Configuration.** Choose the button
  `Start with empty configuration`. The textbox `Editor content` appears.
- **Turn off Auto-generate PCB & 3D.** Choose `Show settings panel`. The
  checkbox `Enable auto-generate PCB and 3D (slow)` is checked. Choose the text
  `Auto-generate PCB & 3D`, and the checkbox is not checked. Choose
  `Hide settings panel`.
- **Paste the outline plate and the PCB board.** Paste a configuration with
  the outline `plate` and the PCB `board`, whose edge is `plate`. The label
  `Download plate.dxf` appears within 30 seconds, no error banner shows, and
  `Download board.kicad_pcb` is not there. `observed.autoGenerated` lists the
  outputs.
- **Choose Generate.** Choose the button `Generate configuration`. The label
  `Download board.kicad_pcb` appears within 30 seconds.
  `observed.generated` lists the outputs.
- **Reload the page.** Choose `Show settings panel`. The checkbox
  `Enable auto-generate PCB and 3D (slow)` is still not checked.
- **Add a custom outline in the settings.** Choose the tab `Outlines`, then
  `Add new custom outline`. The link `download injection custom_outline_1`
  shows in Custom Libraries.
- **Open the address with ?ff_outlines=false.** Go to `/?ff_outlines=false`.
  The warning about skipped custom outlines shows within 30 seconds. In the
  settings, the tab `Footprints` shows and the tab `Outlines` does not.
- **Proof.** `reports/verify/settings/` holds a screenshot and an ARIA snapshot
  after each step, and `result.json` with the observed values.

The journey does not drive `settings-autogen`, `settings-kicad-preview`,
`settings-stl-preview`, `flag-templates`, a flag forced on, or a build
variable. [Preview](./preview.md) drives `settings-debug`. Do not report the
others as verified by this journey.

`pnpm test:e2e` covers part of the feature too. `e2e/ergogen.spec.ts` checks
the STL preview with `STL Preview` and `Debug` stored before the page loads,
and `e2e/share-link.spec.ts` opens a share link with `Auto-generate` stored
off. `src/utils/featureFlags.test.ts` checks the order of the sources of a
flag without a browser.

## Gotchas

- The app does not generate while the settings show. Choosing
  `Hide settings panel` generates with the settings of that moment.
- To prove that the automatic generation left out an output, wait for an
  output that the same generation makes, such as `plate.dxf`, and then check
  that the other one is not there. Empty Configuration lists `demo.dxf`
  already, so do not wait for that one.
- A switch is a checkbox without a box on the screen. Choose its title, such as
  the text `Debug`, and read its state from the checkbox `Enable debug mode`.
- The app reads a flag from the address of the page each time it needs it, and
  stores none. The flag holds only while the address has it.
- The warning about skipped outlines shows only while Custom Libraries holds an
  outline. `Add new custom outline` adds one with a code skeleton, which is
  enough.
